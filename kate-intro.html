Kate, before you read this, I want to share why I felt compelled to write it.

I sat down and took a long, honest look inward, not because I was searching for answers and not because I wanted anything from you, but because I realized how rare it is to recognize when someone has quietly mattered to us. And once I understood that, I didn't want to let it pass unspoken.

This came from a place of gratitude and clarity. From wanting to honor a kindness that may have felt ordinary to you, but wasn't to me. I wrote it slowly, carefully, because I wanted to be truthful without placing any weight on your shoulders.

I then worked together with a digital artist for a few weeks to create this comic and now he has delivered the final result to me.

You don't owe me anything in reading this. I simply wanted you to know what your presence meant, in the only way I know how: by putting it into words and pictures and offering them to you, openly.

So this is the story about me and the most beautiful thing you have done for me.
