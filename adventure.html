One more thing, Kate.

My dearest Kate,

There are so many things I could wish for you on your birthday. But before I do, there are a few things about you that I hope you know.

You are so much more than the beautiful, sexy, funny woman everyone first gets to see.

You are determined and creative. When you want something, you work for it, learn, experiment, change things and keep going until what you imagined becomes real. You care deeply about what you create, and it shows in every detail. You bring so much thought, patience and imagination to the things you make, and watching your ideas slowly take shape is something genuinely special.

I hope you know how much there is to be proud of, Kate.

Because this year you have done so much.

You’ve tried new things. You’ve learned new things. You’ve taken risks. You’ve made difficult decisions. You’ve built something that is completely your own, and you’ve kept going even on days when you were tired, frustrated or unsure whether something would work.

And through all of that, you’ve stayed true to yourself.

And behind everything you do is the girl who gets ridiculously excited about a new monitor, loves sushi enough to moan while eating it, gets completely absorbed in a game, adores her cat and loves her bestie, gets grumpy sometimes, occasionally creates absolute chaos, laughs at my stupid jokes and continues to have deeply questionable opinions about burger sauce.

You are warm, stubborn, silly, ambitious, caring and stronger than you sometimes realize. And sometimes you apparently decide that three words and an emoji contain all the information another human being could possibly need.

So for your birthday, I hope the year ahead gives you plenty of reasons to be proud of yourself.

I hope you create things that excite you and succeed at things you’re currently not even sure you can do. I hope some of the things you dream about today become ordinary parts of your life, and that when they do, you find new things to dream about.

I hope you travel, discover new places, play games until far too late, eat ridiculous amounts of sushi, spend wonderful evenings with your bestie, cuddle Oscar, laugh until your stomach hurts and occasionally manage to spend an entire day without thinking that you should be working on something.

And somewhere among all those big and little things, I hope you always remain wonderfully, unmistakably Kate.

Because that’s the person who became so incredibly important to me.

Not just beautiful Kate. Not just sexy Kate. Not just the creator or the streamer.

All of you, Kate.

The person whose excitement I can sometimes recognize in two words. Whose "Good morning"´s became part of my mornings and "Goodnight"´s accompany me to bed. Whose successes make me happy, whose worries I care about, and whose little everyday moments somehow became important to me too.

And after everything you’ve already allowed me to know about you, there are still so many things I don’t know.

If there are a thousand little pieces of Kate I haven’t discovered yet, I hope you’ll give me the privilege of discovering some of them along the way.

Happy birthday, Kate. ❤️

With all my love,

Dee
